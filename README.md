# Goasante

### Building technology that brings people closer.

I’m the creator of **Mad Buddy**, a privacy-first social app designed to turn nearby friendship into real-world connection. My focus is on useful products, clear experiences, and thoughtful privacy controls.

## Featured project · Mad Buddy

**When your friends are close, they glow.**

Mad Buddy helps approved friends — Muddies — notice when they are nearby, make Plans, coordinate Events, and stay connected through private messages and Groups. Safe Arrival, temporary Stories, and local Conference conversations support everyday connection.

The goal is simple: less endless scrolling, more time together. Nearby experiences use privacy-safe signals without exposing exact locations or location history to other users.

[Explore Mad Buddy](https://mad-buddy.com) · [View the repository](https://github.com/Goasante/mad-buddy)
